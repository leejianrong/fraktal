# Competitors for the studio: Another Earth and Bifrost AI

Checked 2026-11-05 for [ADR-0011](../adr/0011-a-data-generation-studio-as-the-product.md). Claims are the
companies' own or press copy of them, not verified by us. Neither company publishes prices.

## Another Earth (anotherearth.ai)

| | |
|---|---|
| What it sells | Synthetic Earth-observation data (satellite, drone, aerial) and a model fine-tuned to a customer's sensor, region and resolution |
| How it sells | Sales-led: sector pages, "Custom Projects", calls to action are "explore" and "contact us" |
| Shown on the site | No pricing, no named customers, no self-serve tool |
| Claims | Up to 95% less real data needed, about 20% better models (their page; no method or data shown) |
| Fit with us | ADR-0007: datasets and custom projects. Same product shape, and it covers satellite only |

Source: <https://anotherearth.ai/>

## Bifrost AI

| | |
|---|---|
| What it sells | *Stardust*, a platform for AI developers with no simulation team to build simulated 3D environments and generate labelled training data. Robotics, vehicles, aerospace, defence |
| How it sells | Secondary sources describe annual platform subscriptions, usage-based pricing for data volume, and services. Not stated on Bifrost's own pages that we saw |
| Funding | $8M Series A, October 2024 |
| Not covered | Satellite imagery; the sources describe ground and robotics scenes |
| Fit with us | The studio of ADR-0011, minus the satellite side |

Sources:
- Press release: <https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/release/202411019210>
- Profile: <https://www.caplight.com/company/bifrost>
- Coverage: <https://aibusiness.com/generative-ai/generative-3d-platform-funded-targets-heavy-industries>

## What we take from them

- Start as Another Earth does: custom projects and sample datasets, since there is no UI yet.
- Aim at Bifrost's shape: a platform with usage-based pricing for generated volume, plus services.
- Our difference to test: one world model seen through satellite, drone and ground sensors.
- Open: real customers, prices and the quality of either company's data are unknown to us. Look again
  before any pricing decision.
